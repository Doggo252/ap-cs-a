Orbital Mechanics (9 Math Questions Total)
6 Easy Calculations: 
    1. Space Station Speed
    The International Space Station completes 16 orbits around
    Earth every 24 hours. How many hours does it take to complete
    just 1 orbit? Express your answer as a decimal.

    Answer: 1.5

    2. Satellite Travel Distance
    A weather satellite orbits Earth in a circular path with
    a radius of 7,000 km from the Earth's center. Using the
    formula C = 2 * pi * r and approximating pi at 3.14,
    what is the total distance the satellite travels in one full orbit?

    Answer: 43960

    3. Rover Signal Delay
    Radio signals traveling from a Mars orbiter to Earth
    travel at 300,000 km/s. If Mars is currently 150,000,000 km
    away from Earth, how many seconds does it take for a signal
    to reach Earth? Convert your answer to minutes.

    8.33

    4. Lunar Module Fuel Consumption
    A lunar lander uses 45 kg of fuel for every 3 minutes
    of thruster burns during an orbit adjustment. At this
    rate, how many kilograms of fuel will it burn during a
    12-minute maneuver?

    180

    5. Gravity Ratio
    An astronaut weighs 180 lbs on Earth. Due to weaker gravity,
    a person's weight on the Moon is about 1/6 of their weight
    on Earth. How much would the astronaut weigh on the Moon?

    30

    6. Probe Velocity Adjustment
    A deep-space probe is traveling at 12,400 m/s. Flight controllers
    fire its engines to increase its speed by 15%. What is the probe's
    new speed in meters per second?

    14260

3 Hard Calculations:
    1. Transfer Orbit Timing
    A spacecraft enters a transfer orbit to move from a lower orbit to
    a higher orbit. The entire elliptical transfer path takes 14 hours
    to complete a full loop. However, the spacecraft only needs to travel
    along half of the loop to reach the target orbit. Once it arrives, it
    must perform a 25-minute engine burn to lock into place. What is the
    total time in minutes from the start of the transfer to the end of the
    engine burn?

    445

    2. Scale Model Rocket
    Engineers are building a scale model of an orbital launch vehicle.
    The real rocket stands 70 meters tall and has a main engine nozzle
    diameter of 2.8 meters. If the scale model is built so that its height
    is 35 centimeters, what should the diameter of the model's engine nozzle
    be in centimeters?

    1.4

    3. Orbital Decay Loss
    A satellite in low Earth orbit experiences atmospheric drag, causing
    its altitude to decrease by 1.5 km every week.
    
    Part A: If the satellite starts at an altitude of 400 km, what will
            its altitude be after 8 weeks?
            388km
            
    Part B: If ground control fires the boosters to raise the altitude
    back up by 20% from its new 8-week altitude, what is the final altitude?
        465.6
